### **Answers to Answerable Questions**

1. **What's the best diet to lower my A1C?**
   There's no single best diet. Several eating patterns like Mediterranean, low-carb, and vegetarian diets can reduce A1C. The key is reducing overall carbs, increasing fiber, and choosing whole foods over processed ones.

2. **Can losing weight help reverse my type 2 diabetes?**
   Yes, weight loss of 5% or more improves A1C, and losing 15% or more can even lead to remission of type 2 diabetes.

3. **Is it okay to drink alcohol if I have diabetes?**
   Yes, in moderation: up to one drink a day for women and two for men. It's important to monitor for delayed hypoglycemia, especially if using insulin.

4. **Will intermittent fasting help my blood sugar?**
   Intermittent fasting may help with weight loss but hasn't consistently shown better A1C reduction compared to regular calorie restriction.

5. **How many carbs should I eat with type 2 diabetes?**
   There's no set number. Carb intake should be individualized, but reducing overall carbs can help manage blood sugar.

6. **Are low-carb diets safe for diabetics?**
   Low-carb diets are effective for reducing A1C and weight, particularly in type 2 diabetes. However, people should work with a healthcare provider to adjust medications when lowering carbs.

7. **Can a vegan diet help prevent diabetes?**
   Yes, vegetarian and vegan diets are associated with reduced diabetes risk and can lower A1C slightly when adopted by people with diabetes.

8. **Is the Mediterranean diet good for diabetics?**
   Yes, it can lower A1C, improve lipids, and reduce the risk of cardiovascular events.

9. **Does eating more fiber help with diabetes?**
   Yes, fiber can help lower A1C modestly and is associated with reduced mortality in people with diabetes.

10. **Are sugar substitutes like stevia safe for diabetes?**
    Yes, they are approved by the FDA. They can help reduce calorie and carb intake, though long-term benefits on weight or A1C aren't proven.

11. **Should I stop eating fat to prevent diabetes?**
    No. Replacing saturated fat with unsaturated fat can reduce diabetes risk. Total fat isn't as important as fat quality.

12. **Is weight loss important if I have prediabetes?**
    Yes, losing 7–10% of body weight can reduce the risk of progressing to type 2 diabetes.

13. **Can eating too much protein harm me if I'm diabetic?**
    There's no strong evidence of harm, but very high protein diets may not improve diabetes outcomes beyond moderate increases.

14. **How does alcohol affect blood sugar at night?**
    Alcohol can cause delayed hypoglycemia, especially overnight, if consumed in the evening and particularly if on insulin.

15. **Will a low-fat diet help control diabetes?**
    A low-fat diet can help with weight loss, which can improve A1C, but low-fat diets alone don't consistently improve blood sugar or cardiovascular risk factors.

16. **Is there a perfect macronutrient ratio for diabetes?**
    No, macronutrient needs should be individualized. The quality of the carbs, fats, and proteins is more important than exact percentages.

17. **Can I manage diabetes without medication if I lose enough weight?**
    Possibly. Weight loss can lead to remission of type 2 diabetes, reducing or even eliminating the need for medication.

18. **Should I avoid fruit because of the sugar?**
    No. Whole fruits provide fiber and nutrients and can be part of a healthy diet for diabetes.

19. **Does eating more whole grains help with blood sugar?**
    Yes, whole grains are recommended over refined grains and can help improve blood sugar control.

20. **Can fiber supplements lower blood sugar?**
    Yes, high-fiber intake, including through supplements, can modestly lower A1C.

21. **Will drinking diet soda cause diabetes?**
    Some studies show a slight increased risk with diet soda, but replacing sugary drinks with diet soda is still better than regular soda for blood sugar control.

22. **Can skipping meals cause blood sugar problems?**
    Yes, irregular eating can cause blood sugar variability, especially if on medications like insulin.

23. **Can I have cheat days if I'm diabetic?**
    The articles don't address "cheat days" specifically, but consistency in diet is important for blood sugar control.

24. **Do artificial sweeteners increase diabetes risk?**
    Some observational studies suggest a small risk, but evidence is not conclusive. They are FDA-approved for use in diabetes.

25. **Is drinking wine okay if I'm prediabetic?**
    Yes, moderate alcohol, including wine, may reduce diabetes risk, but benefits peak at around one drink per day.

26. **Can high cholesterol in my diet worsen diabetes?**
    The link is unclear. Dietary cholesterol doesn't directly increase cardiovascular events in people with diabetes, but more research is needed.

27. **Should I limit eggs if I have diabetes?**
    Not necessarily, though moderation is advised. The relationship between cholesterol in eggs and diabetes complications isn't fully clear.

28. **Is it bad to eat saturated fat with diabetes?**
    Yes, reducing saturated fat and replacing it with unsaturated fats can lower diabetes and heart disease risks.

29. **Can I get off insulin if I lose weight?**
    Weight loss can reduce or eliminate the need for insulin in type 2 diabetes, especially if the weight loss is substantial.

30. **Will reducing carbs help reduce diabetes meds?**
    Yes, lowering carb intake often reduces the need for glucose-lowering medications.

31. **Can eating patterns prevent prediabetes from becoming diabetes?**
    Yes, eating patterns like the Mediterranean diet can help prevent progression to type 2 diabetes.

32. **Is weight loss surgery good for people with diabetes?**
    Yes, metabolic surgery can lead to significant weight loss and diabetes improvement or remission in some cases.

33. **Are plant-based diets better than low-carb for diabetes?**
    Both can be effective; plant-based diets help with overall health and A1C, while low-carb diets may reduce the need for medication faster.

34. **How much exercise should I do to prevent diabetes?**
    At least 150 minutes per week of moderate-intensity activity is recommended to prevent type 2 diabetes.

35. **Is eating after 8pm bad for blood sugar?**
    The articles don't specify a time, but eating late, especially carb-heavy meals, can elevate morning blood sugar.

36. **Can personalized diets based on genetics help with diabetes?**
    Currently, evidence for genetics-based personalized diets for diabetes is lacking.

37. **What eating pattern lowers diabetes risk the most?**
    Mediterranean, vegetarian, and DASH diets are all associated with lower diabetes risk.

38. **Are nuts good for controlling blood sugar?**
    Yes, nuts are part of the Mediterranean diet and can improve cardiovascular health and blood sugar control.

39. **Is it dangerous to fast with type 1 diabetes?**
    Fasting can be risky due to hypoglycemia, especially without proper medical supervision.

40. **Can I still drink beer with diabetes?**
    Yes, in moderation, though beer contains carbs that can raise blood sugar.

41. **Should I take vitamin D for diabetes?**
    Routine vitamin D supplementation isn't recommended specifically for glycemic control unless deficient.

42. **Do people with diabetes need more protein?**
    No special increase is required unless for weight loss or specific health needs.

43. **Does sodium affect diabetes?**
    Lowering sodium can help reduce blood pressure, which is often elevated in people with diabetes.

44. **Will a low-GI diet lower my A1C?**
    Evidence is mixed, but choosing low-GI carbs may help some people with A1C.

45. **Can losing just 5% of my weight help?**
    Yes, losing 5% of body weight improves A1C, lipids, and blood pressure.

46. **How much fiber should I eat daily with diabetes?**
    At least 14g of fiber per 1,000 calories, with half of grains being whole grains.

47. **Will cutting sugar alone fix my blood sugar?**
    No, it's part of the solution, but total carbs, weight, and physical activity matter too.

48. **Do I need to monitor glucose more if I drink alcohol?**
    Yes, especially overnight, because alcohol can cause delayed hypoglycemia.

49. **What's better for diabetes: low-carb or Mediterranean?**
    Both can help; low-carb may lower A1C faster, but Mediterranean has broader heart benefits.

50. **Does meal timing affect blood sugar?**
    Yes, timing, especially avoiding large late-night meals, can influence blood sugar trends.
